The problem

F-commerce sellers manage
millions of orders by hand.

Notebooks. WhatsApp groups. Excel sheets — always wrong. The result: lost orders, angry customers, no idea of your actual profit.

Orders lost in inbox

Taking orders through Facebook messages means orders get buried, forgotten, or duplicated. No order ID, no status trail.

Stock counts are guesswork

Excel sheets updated manually — if remembered at all. Selling out of stock you don't have. Stockouts you don't know about.

No idea of actual profit

Revenue minus bKash transfers doesn't equal profit. You don't know your best product, peak time, or real return rate.

Customer service by phone call

Customers ask "where is my order?" You spend 30 minutes a day calling couriers to find out. There's no tracking link to share.

Payment chaos

bKash, Nagad, cash on delivery — manually tracked in different notebooks. Who paid? Who didn't? You check every morning.

No receipts, no trust

No proof of purchase for your customers. No professional receipt. Nothing to show when a dispute happens.

The fix

PageConnect replaces all of it — in one mobile-first app.

One place for every order, every customer, every product, every payment — with a public tracking portal your customers can use right now.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is stored on Supabase (enterprise PostgreSQL). Each seller's data is completely isolated — other sellers cannot see your orders or customers. We never share or sell data.

What happens after my trial ends?

You get a 7-day grace period after your 14-day trial to subscribe. After that, premium features lock — but your data is never deleted. You choose when to subscribe.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Start your 14-day trial with just your email and shop name. No payment information needed until you decide to subscribe.

Is there a limit on orders or products?

No limits. All plans — including Starter — have unlimited orders and unlimited products. We don't charge per order or per product.

Can I use PageConnect outside Bangladesh?

Yes. We have a dedicated international seller mode for sellers in Australia, UK, USA, Canada, Germany and more. Switch at signup or from your settings.

What is F-commerce?

F-commerce stands for Facebook commerce — selling products through Facebook pages, groups, WhatsApp and social media rather than a traditional website or marketplace.

Can my customers track their orders?

Yes — from day one. Every seller gets a free public tracking portal at pageconnect.io/track. No setup needed. Customers enter their phone to see all active orders.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel anytime from your account settings. Annual plans: pro-rated refund may be considered within the first 30 days.
