Why PageConnect is different

We're not a marketplace. We don't ask you to change a thing.

Other platforms want you on their terms — list your products their way, take orders their way, lose the direct conversation with your customer. PageConnect works differently. Your Facebook page stays exactly as it is. Your customers still DM you, comment, and chat with you directly. That human connection is your biggest advantage — we'd never take it away. We just help you manage everything that comes after.

The problem

F-commerce sellers manage thousands of orders — by hand.

Notebooks. WhatsApp groups. Excel sheets — always wrong. The result: spending way too much time, lost orders, angry customers, no idea of actual profit, and no data to grow from.

Orders lost in inbox

Taking orders through Facebook messages means orders get buried, forgotten, or duplicated. No order ID, no status trail.

Stock counts are guesswork

Excel sheets updated manually — if remembered at all. Selling out of stock you don't have. Stockouts you don't know about.

No idea of actual profit

Revenue minus payment and courier charges doesn't equal profit. You don't know your best product, peak time, or real return rate.

Customer service by phone call

Customers ask "where is my order?" You spend 30 minutes a day calling couriers to find out. There's no tracking link to share.

Payment chaos

Payment methods manually tracked across different notebooks. Who paid? Who didn't? You check every morning.

No receipts, no trust

No proof of purchase for your customers. No professional receipt. Nothing to show when a dispute happens.

The fix

PageConnect replaces the chaos — in one platform that works beautifully on your phone.

Everything in one place — orders, customers, stock, payments, and a free tracking portal your customers can use right now. Open in your browser, no download, no install.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is stored on Supabase (enterprise PostgreSQL). Each seller's data is completely isolated — other sellers cannot see your orders, customers, or products. We never share or sell data.

What happens after my trial ends?

You get a 3-day grace period after the 14-day trial. After that, premium features lock — but your data is never deleted. You choose when to subscribe.

Do I need to change how I take orders?

No. You keep selling via Facebook inbox, WhatsApp, DMs — exactly as you always have. You just log the order in PageConnect afterwards.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Sign up with your Facebook page name or shop name and email. No payment information needed until you subscribe after the trial.

Can I use PageConnect outside Bangladesh?

Yes. We have a dedicated international seller mode for sellers in Australia, UK, USA, Canada, Germany and more — with USD pricing and full international courier support.

What is F-commerce?

F-commerce stands for Facebook commerce — selling products through Facebook pages, groups, WhatsApp and social media, rather than a traditional marketplace.

Can customers track orders without an account?

Yes. Customers go to pageconnect.io/track, enter their phone number, and see active orders immediately. For full history, receipts, and the seller directory, a free account is needed.

Is this an app I need to download?

No download needed. PageConnect is a web-based platform, fully responsive and optimised for mobile browsers. Open it on your phone and you're ready.

Does PageConnect integrate with couriers automatically?

Not automatically. You enter the tracking number when you have it — customers can then click directly through to the courier's own tracking page. Your courier relationships stay as they are.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel anytime from your account settings. Contact help@pageconnect.io for billing questions.
