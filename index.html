The problem

F-commerce sellers manage thousands of orders — by hand.

Notebooks. WhatsApp groups. Excel sheets — always wrong. The result: lost orders, angry customers, no idea of actual profit, and no data to grow from.

Orders lost in inbox

Facebook messages get buried, forgotten, or duplicated. No order ID, no status trail, no way to prove what was ordered.

Stock counts are guesswork

Excel sheets updated manually — if remembered. Stockouts happen without warning. Overselling is constant.

No idea of actual profit

Revenue minus courier charges is not your profit. Packaging, time, returns — you have no visibility of the real number.

Customer service by phone call

"Where is my order?" — 30 minutes a day calling couriers and checking notebooks. No tracking link to share.

Payment chaos

Bkash here, cash there, advance paid but forgotten. Who paid? Who didn't? You check every single morning.

No receipts, no trust

No proof of purchase. No professional receipt. Nothing to show when a dispute happens with a customer or courier.

The fix

PageConnect replaces the chaos — in one platform that works beautifully on your phone.

Everything in one place — orders, customers, stock, payments, analytics, and a free tracking portal your customers can use from day one. Open in your browser on any device. No download, no install, no technical setup.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is stored on Supabase (enterprise PostgreSQL). Each seller's data is completely isolated from others. We never share or sell your data — ever.

What happens after my trial ends?

You get a 3-day grace period after the 7-day trial. After that, premium features lock. Your data is retained for up to 2 years of inactivity, after which it may be permanently deleted. You can export your data at any time.

Do I need to change how I take orders?

No. Keep selling via Facebook inbox, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs — exactly as you always have. You just log the order in PageConnect afterwards.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Sign up with your Facebook page name or shop name and email. No payment needed until you subscribe after the trial.

Can I use PageConnect outside Bangladesh?

Yes. We have a dedicated international seller mode for sellers in Australia, UK, USA, Canada, Germany and more — with USD pricing and full international courier support.

What is F-commerce?

F-commerce stands for Facebook commerce — selling products through Facebook pages, groups, WhatsApp, Instagram and social media, rather than a traditional marketplace.

Can customers track orders without an account?

Yes. Go to pageconnect.io/track, enter phone number, see active orders. For full history, receipts, and the seller directory, a free customer account is needed.

Is this an app I need to download?

No download needed. PageConnect is fully web-based and responsive for mobile browsers. Open on your phone in any browser and you're ready.

Does PageConnect integrate with couriers automatically?

Not automatically. You enter the courier tracking number when you have it — customers then click through to the courier's own tracking page. Your courier relationships stay exactly as they are.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel anytime from account settings. Contact help@pageconnect.io for any billing questions.

Can PageConnect change pricing or features?

Yes. PageConnect reserves the right to update pricing, features, policies, and terms at any time. We will notify registered users of material changes by email. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.

How long is my data kept?

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. For inactive accounts, data is kept for up to 2 years, after which it may be permanently deleted. You can export all your data at any time from your account settings.
