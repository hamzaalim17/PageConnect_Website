Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 May 2026  ·  Last updated: 19 May 2026

PageConnect is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We do not sell your data — ever.

1. Who we are

PageConnect ("we", "us", "our") operates the platform at pageconnect.io, including the seller dashboard, customer portal, and public tracking portal. Our contact email is help@pageconnect.io.

2. What data we collect

From sellers:

  • Name, email address, and shop name at registration
  • Order data you enter — customer names, phone numbers, addresses, products, prices, and delivery details
  • Payment method records you log manually
  • Product and stock information you add
  • Financial records you enter in the Finance section

From customers (registered):

  • Email address at registration
  • Order history linked to their phone number across PageConnect sellers

From customers (unregistered):

  • Phone number entered at pageconnect.io/track to retrieve active orders — not stored permanently

Automatically collected:

  • Browser type, device type, and general usage data for platform performance
  • We do not use invasive tracking or advertising cookies

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and operate the PageConnect platform
  • To allow sellers to manage orders, customers, stock, and analytics
  • To allow customers to track orders and view purchase history
  • To send transactional emails related to your account (trial expiry, billing)
  • To improve the platform based on usage patterns

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Data storage and security

All data is stored on Supabase — an enterprise-grade PostgreSQL database with row-level security. Each seller's data is completely isolated. No seller can access another seller's orders, customers, or products.

We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Access to production data is strictly limited.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Supabase (database hosting) and Vercel (platform hosting) — both bound by their own privacy policies
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Business transfer: If PageConnect is acquired or merged, data may transfer to the new entity under the same privacy commitments

6. Cookies

PageConnect uses only essential cookies required for the platform to function — session management and authentication. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies from third parties.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at help@pageconnect.io. We will respond within 14 business days.

8. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. For inactive accounts, data is retained for up to 2 years of inactivity, after which it may be permanently deleted. You can export all your data at any time from your account settings.

If you wish to request early deletion of your data, contact us at help@pageconnect.io and we will action your request promptly.

9. Children's privacy

PageConnect is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users by email of any material changes. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.

11. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at help@pageconnect.io. We aim to respond within 14 business days.

This Privacy Policy was last updated on 19 May 2026. PageConnect reserves the right to update this policy at any time. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
